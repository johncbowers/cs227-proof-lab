Proof language

End punctuation is optional. References are optional when the compiler can infer exactly which earlier lines you mean.

  • Pretend Bob is a Creature or Let Bob be an arbitrary Creature opens an arbitrary-object world.
  • Pretend X, Y are Numbers or Let X, Y be arbitrary Numbers opens several arbitrary-object worlds at once.
  • Make believe S(Bob) or Assume S(Bob) opens a make-believe world.
  • S(Bob) → M(Bob) by UI I applies a rule and cites an axiom.
  • X < Y → F(X) < F(Y) by UI may instantiate several leading quantifiers at once; the source reference may be omitted when it is unambiguous.
  • M(Bob) by detachment 2, 3 cites proof lines.
  • therefore S(Bob) → V(Bob) by MB 6 closes a make-believe world.
  • therefore (∀ c in Creatures)(S(c) → V(c)) by UG 7 closes an arbitrary-object world.
  • Pretend Ari is a witness with P(Ari) by EI I opens an existential-witness world.
  • Case 1: Make believe P by cases III opens the first branch of a proof by cases. Nested branches may use labels such as Case 1a and Case 1b.
  • After establishing the goal in a branch, therefore R by case finishes that branch. Finish Case 1a before beginning Case 1b.
  • After both sibling branches are finished, therefore R by cases III combines them; the checker finds the two matching branch conclusions.

Compiler convention: quantified variables and definition parameters begin lowercase; introduced individuals such as Bob begin uppercase. A quantifier may omit its type when the surrounding proof determines it; two explicitly different types still conflict.

Several variables may share a quantifier, as in (∀ x, y)(x < y → F(x) < F(y)). Function terms may be nested, as in F(G(x)). Definitions may have several parameters, as in Between(x, y, z).

Other rule names: EI, EG, and introduction, and elimination, or introduction, case, cases, De Morgan, double negation, iff introduction, and iff elimination.

Shorthand becomes notation as you type: \forall, \exists, \neg, /\, \/, ->, <->, <=, and >=. The words and and or are also accepted inside formulas.
